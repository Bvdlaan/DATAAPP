How answers are made

  • The figures come directly from the official databases above, fetched at the moment you ask.
  • The answer text is written by an AI model (Mistral, from France). The AI chooses which table to use, reads the result and explains it in plain language.
  • We instruct the AI to state only numbers that come from the fetched data, never from its own memory, and to name the source, table and period.
  • AI can make mistakes, for example by choosing a table that isn't the most recent. Always check the source named in the answer. If something looks wrong, tap Report a problem under the answer.
  • Different offices sometimes publish different figures for the same topic, for example national inflation versus the EU-harmonised measure. Both can be correct; the answer names which one it uses.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share personal data, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • The app doesn't use cookies, analytics, or tracking. Fonts are served by us, not by third parties.
  • The app isn't aimed at children under 13.
